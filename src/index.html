Coast to Coast: weather on your ride

Set your speed or power, then drag the slider to see where you are on the route at your average speed, and the wind and weather you'll meet there. Weather comes live from the Dutch KNMI model (via Open-Meteo) by default; or the GFS model that Windfinder is based on, or a saved Yr.no forecast.

Forecast data (view, edit or paste)

One row per forecast step: point km, date, hour, wind-from °, wind kts, gust kts, °C, rain mm per 3 h, cloud %. Points without data borrow from the nearest one that has it. The rows of the selected source are shown here (KNMI: hourly, rain = hourly mm × 3); edit them or paste your own and press Apply.
